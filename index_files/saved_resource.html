My story

About Me

My name is Sameeha Sadia Shaik. I was born on June 22, 2007, in Visakhapatnam, India. I grew up with the love and guidance of my mother, Ushnara Shaik, who raised me as a single parent, and the support of my maternal uncles, Iqbal Shaik and Maqbul Shaik.

I attended Bethany School in Visakhapatnam, where I completed my I.C.S.E. in 2023. I then completed my first year of intermediate studies, focusing on Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, with distinction.

My mother married David Yalla in December 2021. In 2024, our family moved to the Minneapolis, Minnesota area, beginning an exciting new chapter in my life. I graduated from Rosemount High School in 2025.

Today, I am pursuing an associate's degree in Computer Science at Inver Hills Community College. I serve as Student Council President and work in the student body office. I hope to transfer to the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, in 2027 to continue my education and explore new opportunities in technology.
